Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026

The short version. The game runs in your browser. The names you type stay in your browser. The only thing we ever hold is an email address, and only if you choose to give us one.

What we collect

  • Your email address — only if you type it into the "join the circle" box, so we can send you new packs and news about the printable deck.
  • Nothing else. There are no accounts, no passwords, no profiles.

The names you type when you play

Player names are used to run the game on your device. They are not sent to us and are not stored on our servers. Close the page and they are gone.

If you buy the printable deck

The payment is taken by Airwallex. Your card details go to Airwallex and are never seen or stored by us. We keep the record of the purchase (what was bought, when, and the email it was sent to) because accounting and tax rules require it.

Cookies and analytics

We set no cookies. Our visitor counting is Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless: it counts visits without identifying anybody.

How long we keep it

Our retention policy, in plain numbers rather than a pointer at the law:

  • Your email address — kept for as long as you want to hear from us, and deleted within 7 days of the day you ask us to remove it. You can ask at any time, and no older copy is kept in the background.
  • Purchase records (what was bought, when, the email it was sent to, and the receipt) — kept for 5 years, because Singapore tax law (IRAS) requires business records to be kept that long. This is the one record we cannot delete on request before then.
  • Nothing else. The names typed when you play never reach us, so there is nothing to keep or remove.

Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act you may ask to see what we hold, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent to us holding it. Write to the address below and we will act on it.

How to have your data deleted

One email does it. Write to danielg@kylyai.com with delete my data in the subject line — no form to fill in, no account to make, and no reason needed. It costs you nothing and we will not ask why.

  • We confirm we have your request the same working day, Singapore time.
  • Your email address is deleted within 7 days and taken off every list we hold.
  • If one record has to stay, we tell you in writing and name it — that is the purchase record described in "How long we keep it" above, kept for 5 years because Singapore tax law (IRAS) requires it. Nothing else of yours is kept.

Seeing it or putting it right works the same way: ask in the same email.

Where your data goes

Almost everything about HushSide stays on your own device. The two things that do travel are handled outside Singapore:

  • Vercel (United States) — hosts this website and the serverless function that receives your email address when you use the join-the-circle form. Their servers are in the United States.
  • Cloudflare (United States) — the cookieless visit counter that counts how many people read this page, without identifying anybody.
  • Telegram — when you use the join-the-circle form, the serverless function sends your email address to Telegram's API so that signup reaches Daniel's private channel. Telegram is operated by Telegram Messenger Inc., a company incorporated in the British Virgin Islands, with servers worldwide.
  • Airwallex (United States) — processes the payment when you buy the printable deck. Your card details go directly to Airwallex and never reach our servers.

Your data leaving Singapore — what that means for you

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), personal data may only be sent overseas when the recipient is bound to protect it to the same standard Singapore law requires. We choose providers that do that, and we stay responsible for your data while they hold it.

The consent that covers each transfer is the act of using the join form or making a purchase. You may withdraw that consent at any time by emailing danielg@kylyai.com with withdraw consent in the subject line — we confirm the same working day and delete your data within seven days. The withdrawal takes effect from the day we confirm it.

Children

HushSide is written for grown-ups and older teenagers, and it is not a children's product. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
